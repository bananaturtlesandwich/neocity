oh boy...who the hell am i? this has probably been one of the pages i've been putting off properly doing because online strangers will base a large part of their perception of you on it. while i've never been one to care whether people think i'm weird or obtuse, i do care i present my authentic self without compromise. okay lets do this thing...

you can call me spuds! i'm the current resident of this here shack. not a native of the internet void, instead i came into existence in the UK at some period soon after the mid-2000s. allegedly this means i have become an adult which will likely not sink in for another decade. definitely won't look it for a bit either due to my half-chinese genetics.

at the moment i continue to have few responsibilities for a bit longer thanks to university. since i'm indecisive, i'm doing natural sciences but am starting to specialise into either developmental biology or zoology. i really love both fields but am completely clueless what to do with myself after graduation. aside from that i have a lot of fun playing accordion in the ceilidh band and helping with tech for theatre shows.

as for my more solitary hobbies i really enjoy making art, playing games, watching shows/films, reading books, folding origami, making this website and coding (mostly to mod said games but i've been trying out some game dev). the trouble is that my automatic response whenever i have a sliver of free time is to watch youtube so i'm bad at engaging with these consistently :(. well i'm writing this now! at least that's something :).
